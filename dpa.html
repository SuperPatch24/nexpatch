Data Processing Agreement

Version 2026-11-15 · Last updated November 15, 2026

In short: This agreement applies when your organization uses NexpatchOnline to process personal data from its Microsoft tenant. Your organization is the controller, NexpatchOnline is the processor, and we use the data only to run the service for you.

1. Scope and parties

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is between the organization that accepts it ("Customer") and NexpatchOnline ("Processor"). It applies to personal data in Customer Data that Processor processes for Customer under the Terms of Service, and forms part of them. Terms like "controller", "processor", "personal data" and "sub-processor" have the meaning given in the GDPR and similar data protection laws ("Data Protection Law").

2. Roles and instructions

Customer is the controller and Processor is the processor. Processor will process personal data only on Customer's documented instructions, which are these terms, the Customer's use and configuration of the Service, and other written instructions that are consistent with the Service. Processor will tell Customer if it believes an instruction breaks Data Protection Law. Customer is responsible for having a lawful basis and giving any notices needed for its data.

4. Confidentiality

Processor will make sure the people who can access personal data are bound by confidentiality and have access only as needed to provide the Service.

5. Security

Processor will keep reasonable technical and organizational measures suited to the risk, including: encryption in transit and at rest; separation of each customer's data; role-based access with sign-in through Microsoft Entra; per-device credentials and rate limits for the App Control agent; audit logging; and secrets kept out of source code. Processor may update these measures if the level of protection is not reduced.

7. Help with Customer's duties

Taking into account the nature of the processing, Processor will reasonably help Customer to answer requests from individuals, to carry out data protection assessments, and to deal with authorities. Processor may charge reasonable costs for help beyond what the Service already provides. If an individual contacts Processor directly about Customer Data, Processor will refer them to Customer.

8. Personal data breaches

Processor will tell Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a breach of security affecting Customer's personal data, and will give the information it has to help Customer meet its own duties. A notice is not an admission of fault.

9. Return and deletion

Customer can export reports from the Service at any time. After the subscription ends, or on Customer's written request, Processor will delete Customer Data within 90 days, unless the law requires it to keep some. Backups are deleted on their normal schedule.

10. Audits

Processor will give Customer the information reasonably needed to show it follows this DPA, such as answers to security questions and summaries of its measures. If that is not enough, Customer may carry out an audit, no more than once a year, with 30 days' written notice, during business hours, under confidentiality, at Customer's cost, and without access to other customers' data.

11. International transfers

Processor may transfer personal data to countries outside Customer's region, including through its sub-processors. Where Data Protection Law requires it, the parties agree that the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission (Module 2, controller to processor) and, for the United Kingdom, the UK addendum, are included in this DPA. They apply only to the extent such a transfer needs them.

12. Liability and order of precedence

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limits in the Terms of Service. If this DPA conflicts with the Terms of Service about personal data, this DPA wins. Processor's contact for this DPA is legal@nexpatchonline.com.

13. Acceptance

This DPA takes effect when an authorized administrator of Customer accepts it in the Service. The date and person are recorded.
