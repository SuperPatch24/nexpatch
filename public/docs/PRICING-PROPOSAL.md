# NexpatchOnline proposed launch pricing

These are suggested public list prices in USD, not active billing or subscription enforcement.

| Plan | Monthly | Device allowance |
|---|---:|---:|
| Starter | $49 | 50 |
| Growth | $149 | 250 |
| Business | $399 | 1,000 |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom |

Suggested annual discount: 15%. Keep the first pricing release simple, include core patching and documentation in all paid tiers, and reserve advanced App Control, audit history, custom onboarding and priority support for higher tiers.

Benchmark context: Patch My PC advertises a $3,500/year starting minimum for up to 1,000 devices, while Action1 advertises its first 200 endpoints free. NexpatchOnline should compete on a low minimum commitment for smaller Intune environments and clear integrated patching/App Control workflows rather than trying to match a free endpoint offer. These competitor offers may change; re-check them before publishing final terms.

Do not activate checkout or promise these plan entitlements until subscription metering, billing, overage handling, cancellation, tax and support processes are implemented.
