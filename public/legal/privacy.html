Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-15 · Last updated November 15, 2026

In short: We collect what we need to run the Service: your account details, and the device and app information from your Microsoft tenant. We don't sell your data and we don't run ads or tracking. Your organization is in control of the data from its tenant, and we handle it on its behalf.

1. Who we are

NexpatchOnline runs NexpatchOnline. For privacy questions write to legal@nexpatchonline.com.

2. Two roles

  • Data from your organization's tenant (devices, apps, events). Your organization decides why and how it is used, and we process it on its behalf under the Data Processing Agreement. If you are an employee of a customer, your IT department is the first contact for questions about this data.
  • Account, billing, website and security data. We decide how this is used, so we are the controller.

4. How we use it

To sign you in and control access; to show your inventory and reports; to prepare and deploy packages when you ask; to run App Control reports; to send service messages (invitations, alerts, invoices); to keep the Service secure, fix problems and prevent abuse; to provide support; to bill; and to meet legal obligations. We don't sell personal data, and we don't use Customer Data for advertising.

5. Who we share it with

Only with service providers that help us run the Service, under contracts that protect the data, and when the law requires it. Today these are Microsoft (Azure hosting and storage, and your own Microsoft tenant), Brevo (email), Cloudflare (DNS, email routing and delivery) and, when billing is switched on, our payment provider. The list is kept in the Data Processing Agreement. If we sell or reorganize the business, data may move to the new owner, which must honor this policy.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and audit records: while your organization uses the Service, then up to 90 days.
  • Device inventory, App Control events and uploaded files: while your organization uses the Service. We delete them within 90 days after it ends, or sooner on your written request.
  • Invoices and tax records: as long as the law requires.
  • Backups and logs: they roll off on a regular schedule after the live data is deleted.

7. Where it is stored

In Microsoft Azure data centers in the region we chose for the Service, and with the providers above, which may process data in other countries. Where required, we use legal safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

8. Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, separation between customers, role-based access, per-device credentials for the agent, and audit logging. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell the affected customer without undue delay.

9. Cookies and local storage

We use only what is needed for sign-in and to remember your settings, such as theme and a demo preference. There are no advertising or analytics trackers.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct, delete, export or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to certain uses, and to complain to your data protection authority. For data from your employer's tenant, contact your IT department; we will help them respond. For account and billing data, write to legal@nexpatchonline.com. We may need to confirm who you are.

11. Children

The Service is for business use and not for children under 16.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here, and for material changes we will ask you to accept it in the Service or tell you by email.
