# NexpatchOnline Privacy Notice — draft for legal review

**Status: Draft template. Complete with actual data flows, retention periods, subprocessors, legal bases and contact details before publishing.**

## Information processed
Depending on enabled features, NexpatchOnline may process account identifiers and work email addresses, organization/tenant identifiers, device and application inventory, deployment assignments and results, support requests, audit events, uploaded package metadata, remediation script metadata, invoices and transactional email delivery records.

## Purposes
Data may be used to authenticate users, connect authorized Microsoft tenants, manage software catalogs and deployments, provide support, generate reports, secure the service, process billing and meet legal obligations.

## Microsoft and other providers
When an organization connects Microsoft Intune, NexpatchOnline accesses tenant data according to the permissions approved by that organization's administrator. Microsoft and other configured service providers process data under their own terms. List all actual hosting, storage, email, telemetry, payment and support subprocessors before publication.

## Data minimization and retention
Define retention separately for account records, device inventory, audit logs, installer uploads, email content, invoices and backups. Provide a deletion/export process and identify legal retention exceptions. Do not claim a retention period until it is implemented and verified.

## Security

## International transfers and rights
